Printing Instructions:

  1. Ensure all pages you want are generated.
  2. Customize your message on the "Inside Right" page if desired.
  3. Click "Print Card".
  4. In your browser's print dialog:
    • Select your printer.
    • Choose "Print on both sides" or "Two-sided".
    • Select "Flip on long edge" (sometimes called "Booklet" or "Flip over").
    • Ensure Layout is "Portrait".
    • Paper size should match your card stock (e.g., Letter, A4).
    • Margins: "Default" or "Minimum" often works well. Preview in the dialog.
    • Scale: "Default" or "100%". Do NOT use "Fit to page" if it shrinks content.
  5. For best results, use card stock paper.
  6. After printing, fold the paper in half along the short edge.
